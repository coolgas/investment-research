## US—Iran Conflict: Ceasefire Extension Reached

- **US and Iran agree to 60-day truce renewal, pending Trump signoff.** The tentative memorandum of understanding extends the ceasefire and opens further talks on Tehran's nuclear program. Treasury Secretary Bessent stated Trump "will not make a bad deal" and Iran must surrender highly enriched uranium and keep the Strait of Hormuz fully open. (Politics, via Axios)
- **Markets rally on the news.** US stocks reversed earlier losses, oil pared gains, Treasuries rose, and the dollar fell against all major peers as risk appetite surged on the prospect of de-escalation after three months of war. (Markets)
- **Oil pares war premium.** Crude pulled back from elevated levels as the ceasefire extension eased supply disruption fears that had driven an oil-fueled inflation surge across global economies. (Politics)
- **Travel stocks soar.** Airlines and travel-related equities jumped sharply as oil prices dropped on the ceasefire headlines. Kayak CEO noted consumers are still booking trips despite geopolitical chaos. (Technology)
